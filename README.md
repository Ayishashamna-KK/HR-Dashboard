# HR Analytics Dashboard & Employee Attrition Analysis
📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Human Resources (HR) data through interactive dashboards to understand employee distribution, workforce performance, attendance, and employee attrition.

The project consists of two dashboards:

HR Dashboard – Provides an overview of the organization's workforce, employee distribution, department performance, employment types, and attendance.

Attrition Analysis Dashboard – Focuses on employee resignations, terminations, attrition percentage, and department-wise workforce trends.

The dashboards help HR professionals identify workforce patterns, monitor employee performance, and make data-driven decisions to improve employee retention.

🎯 Project Objectives

Analyze total and active employee distribution.

Calculate and monitor employee attrition percentage.

Identify departments with higher employee resignations.

Analyze employee distribution by age group and gender.

Compare employee performance across departments.

Evaluate attendance and training hours.

Understand workforce distribution by employment type and location.

Provide meaningful insights to support HR decision-making.

🔍 Key Insights

Employee Attrition: Approximately 24% of the workforce has left the organization.

Employee Retention: 1,899 employees remain active.

Resignations: 480 employees resigned, while 121 employees were terminated.

Department Distribution: IT has the highest employee count, with 375 employees.

Location Analysis: Al Ain has the highest employee count (539), followed by Dubai (507).

Employment Type: Full-time employees represent the largest employment category, with 863 employees.

Department Attrition: Sales has the highest number of resignations (76), followed by IT (75).

Workforce Performance: The dashboards compare average, high, and low performance levels across departments.

📝 Conclusion
